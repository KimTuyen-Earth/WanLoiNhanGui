BUOC 2: CAU HINH EMAILJS
- Truy cap https://www.emailjs.com/

- Tao Email Template.

BUOC 3: CAU HINH EMAIL TEMPLATE
Trong template, su dung cac bien:

Ten nguoi gui: {{sender_name}}
Email nguoi gui: {{sender_email}}
Ten nguoi nhan: {{recipient_name}}
Email nguoi nhan: {{recipient_email}}
Tieu de: {{subject}}
Noi dung: {{message}}

Trong truong To Email cua template:
{{recipient_email}}
